Why it matters which way the grain runs.

CRGO carries magnetic flux best along its rolling direction. That is why transformer core laminations are cut so the flux runs along each lamination's length, and why the corners of a mitre core are cut at 45°. CRNO has no strongly preferred direction, so it suits parts where the flux path keeps changing.
