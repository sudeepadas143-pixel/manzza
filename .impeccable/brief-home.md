# Surface brief: home page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: buyers and engineers at transformer manufacturers in
South India; the founder wants MANZZA to read as the ambitious new name in the
industry. Action: request a quote (send a drawing).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Apple's product-page grammar applied to an industrial component — the
lamination is presented like a flagship product, not a catalogue line. Refuses
the industrial-supplier default (dense spec walls, stock photos, navy boxes).

OWN-WORLD: black ground, graphite #161617 tiles at 28px radius, one family
(Geist) set large, semibold and tight, grey #86868b supporting copy, electric
blue #2f6bea pill for the single action, #6ea2ff chevron links. One bold move:
the product name in brushed-steel gradient.

STORY: the scroll film shows the core being made; the hero names it plainly;
the lineup lets a buyer act or learn per product; materials, process and
capacity build confidence; the close asks for the drawing.

FIRST VIEWPORT (after the film): centred two-tone headline, one-paragraph lede,
blue "Request a quote" pill beside a "See the products ›" link.

FORM: brief-pinned (Apple-esque); no concept roll. Seed key: none (pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
