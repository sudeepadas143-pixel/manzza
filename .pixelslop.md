# Pixelslop — Project Design Context

## Register

brand

## Audience

Transformer manufacturers, core assembly and fabrication shops, and OEM procurement and engineering teams in Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu (B2B, technical buyers)

## Brand

Powerful, innovative, technical, dependable. Premium black and electric-blue industrial design system (client brief); young, ambitious founder

## Aesthetic

SpaceX design language (design/reference/spacex.DESIGN.md) in black, deep navy and electric blue; condensed uppercase Barlow; full-bleed WebGL renders of the laminations; restrained one-shot motion

## Principles

Use only client-approved copy and verified claims; no prices; avoid excessive animation, stock photography, clutter and exaggerated claims

## Off Limits

The scroll intro film (logo to coil to core), the logo artwork, all copy wording (client-approved), the black/navy/electric-blue palette, the SpaceX-derived layout
