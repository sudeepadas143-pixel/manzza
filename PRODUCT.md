# MANZZA Industry Pvt. Ltd. — product truth

## What it is
A Bengaluru manufacturer of transformer core laminations: mitre step-lap (flagship),
E&I and strip laminations, cut, punched and prepared from CRGO and CRNO electrical
steel to each customer's drawing. One job: transformer core laminations, made to drawing.

## Who it serves
Transformer manufacturers, core assembly and fabrication shops, and OEM procurement and
engineering teams across Karnataka, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu.

## What the site must do
Make MANZZA read as the ambitious new name in the industry (the founder is young and
building the brand) while staying precise enough for engineers; get visitors to send a
drawing for a quotation.

## Voice
Plain, specific, confident. Engineering facts, not slogans. Sentence case.

## Constraints and evidence
- No invented numbers, certifications, test claims, testimonials, prices or client logos.
- No competitor names.
- Contact details and the quote-form endpoint are placeholders until MANZZA supplies them
  (`site.config.js`); the 160 MT/month capacity is MANZZA's own indicative figure, shown
  with its note until confirmed.
- The logo is used as supplied (metallic artwork on black).

## Brand commitments
- Colours: steel and near-black for the interface (the client asked for no blue buttons);
  electric blue (#2f6bea) stays in the logo and the intro film.
- Visual direction (pinned by the client): Apple's product-page grammar, dark.
