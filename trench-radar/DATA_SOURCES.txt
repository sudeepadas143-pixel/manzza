THE LIVE PUMP.FUN MEMECOIN FEED (main source)
======================================================================
wss://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com
    Solana's free public websocket. The app sends:
    {"jsonrpc":"2.0","id":1,"method":"logsSubscribe",
     "params":[{"mentions":["6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P"]},
               {"commitment":"confirmed"}]}
    6EF8rrecthR5Dkzon8Nwu78hRvfCKubJ14M5uBEwF6P = the pump.fun program.
    Gives every launch (CreateEvent), trade (TradeEvent), graduation (CompleteEvent)
    and PumpSwap migration (CompletePumpAmmMigrationEvent).
    LIVE CHECK: OK. 35 s -> 1,307 trades + 8 launches (~37 events/s), median lag 1.1 s.

wss://solana-rpc.publicnode.com                         (fallback, same subscription)
    LIVE CHECK: OK. 26 s -> 761 trades + 4 launches (~29 events/s), median lag 1.3 s.

wss://mainnet.helius-rpc.com/?api-key=YOUR_KEY          (optional, steadier; set SOLANA_WS_URL)
    Not checked (needs a key).

Code: radar/stream.py (subscription), radar/sources/pump_events.py (decoder)
